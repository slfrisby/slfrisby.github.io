Our **new paper** is out today in *Imaging Neuroscience*! Recording human intracranical electrocorticography (ECoG) data from the ventral anterior temporal lobe (vATL), we demonstrated that both local computations in the vATL and long-range connections to other regions are important for semantic representation. The paper is available to read [here](https://slfrisby.github.io/assets/pdf/frisby_all_2026.pdf).
