Another **new paper** is out, this time in *Human Brain Mapping*. We compare the capabilities of five ultra-high-field functional MRI (7T-fMRI) sequences for improving image quality in ventral temporal and orbitofrontal regions, concluding that a multi-echo, multiband sequence does best. The paper is available to read [here](https://slfrisby.github.io/assets/pdf/frisby_optimising_2026.pdf).
