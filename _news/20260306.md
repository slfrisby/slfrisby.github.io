I was thrilled to receive another grant, this time from the [Cambridge Language Sciences Incubator Fund](https://www.languagesciences.cam.ac.uk/incubator)! This grant will fund the development of a publicly-available stimulus set for investigating the contributions of different sensory modalities (visually-presented objects, written words, spoken words, and natural sounds) to semantic cognition, as well as an exciting project investigating similarities between neural networks and human behaviour. Watch this space.
